# Onboarding image attribution

These photographs were checked on their Pexels source pages. Each page labels
the photo as free to use and identifies its contributor and subject. The
downloaded JPEGs were resized to 1800 pixels wide through the Pexels image CDN;
the photos were not otherwise edited. Image dimensions and JPEG decoding were
verified locally after download.

Pexels' Content License grants rights to download, use, copy, modify, and adapt
photos for commercial or non-commercial purposes. Attribution is not required;
credits are recorded here. Pexels prohibits redistributing the photos as
standalone files and notes that separate rights may apply to depicted people,
brands, or other protected content. Here the photos are used as backgrounds
combined with Flutter text and controls.
